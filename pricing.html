FAQ

Questions About Pricing

Everything you might want to know before you sign up.

What's actually different between the plans?

Manual and AI-Powered include every feature - AI-Powered just adds an AI CFO and AI Doctor: report summaries, a query assistant, and recommendations. Automatic isn't a tier you can buy; it's a feature we've chosen not to build.

Why isn't the Automatic plan available?

That would mean EnrichMe talking to your bank, CAMS, and KFintech directly on your behalf, via the RBI Account Aggregator framework. We think that access is too risky to build responsibly - by design, not a delay.

How does the 25-per-day AI limit work?

Up to 25 AI queries a day - summaries, questions, and recommendations all count. Resets daily, no monthly cap.

Can I switch between Manual and AI-Powered anytime?

Yes, from your account settings, anytime. No lock-in, no retention calls.

Will the price ever change?

Only if our real infrastructure or AI costs change meaningfully - and we'll tell you exactly why, not raise it quietly.

Is my data secure?

Your document vault is AES-256 encrypted with a passphrase only you know. Account data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Do you sell my data?

No. Never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else. It stays yours.
