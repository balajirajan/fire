FAQ

Questions About Pricing

Everything you might want to know before choosing a plan.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account settings — changes apply from your next billing cycle, and nothing you've already tracked is lost.

Is Basic really free forever?

Yes. Basic isn't a trial — it stays free for as long as you use it, with no card required to sign up.

What exactly is the Account Aggregator integration on HNI?

It's India's RBI/SEBI-regulated framework for securely sharing your financial data across institutions with your explicit consent, so your stocks, mutual funds and bonds show up in EnrichMe without manual entry. You can revoke access anytime.

How many people can share a Family plan?

Up to 5 members, each with their own login, sharing selected goals and a joint expense split — while personal accounts stay private unless explicitly shared.

Is my data secure?

Your document vault uses AES-256 encryption locked with a passphrase only you know — not even we can read it. Account data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
