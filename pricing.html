FAQ

Questions About Pricing

Everything you might want to know before you sign up.

Why isn't EnrichMe completely free?

Running the app costs real money - a cloud database, encrypted storage, and the AI features that read your documents and answer your questions. ₹25/month is our actual per-user cost, split evenly, with nothing added on top for profit.

Are there paid tiers or upgrades?

No. Every EnrichMe user gets every feature - net worth, FIRE planning, insurance, the document vault, will planner, health tracking, all of it. There's no premium tier to upsell you into.

Will the price ever change?

Only if our real infrastructure costs change meaningfully. If it ever does, we'll tell you exactly why, not just raise it quietly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, anytime from your account settings. No lock-in period, no retention calls.

Is my data secure?

Your document vault uses AES-256 encryption locked with a passphrase only you know - not even we can read it. Account data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Do you sell my data?

No. Never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else, for any reason. It stays yours.

Will I get marketing emails or texts?

No. We don't send promotional SMS or marketing email campaigns. You'll only hear from us for essential account activity, and even that stays minimal.
