Without A Will

What the law would decide instead

If a Hindu male dies intestate (without a will), the Hindu Succession Act, 1956 splits his property equally among his Class I heirs - spouse, each surviving child, and mother. No wishes involved, just headcount. Tell us who's alive to see the equal split.
