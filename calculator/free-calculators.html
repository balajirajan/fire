Frequently Asked Questions

Are these financial calculators really free?

Yes - every calculator on this page is completely free, with no paywall, no premium tier, and no usage limits. They're maintained as part of the free EnrichMe app, so there's no catch hidden inside the tools.

Do I need an account or email to use them?

No. Every calculator on this page runs directly in your browser without registration, email, or credit card. If you want to track your progress over time, you can optionally create a free account in the app.

Which FIRE calculator should I start with?

Start with the FIRE Calculator to find your overall FIRE number and timeline. Then use the Coast FIRE Calculator to see when you could stop saving, and the Barista FIRE Calculator if part-time work is part of your plan.

What's the difference between SIP and Step-Up SIP?

A regular SIP invests the same fixed amount every month throughout the tenure. A Step-Up SIP lets you increase your monthly investment by a fixed percentage each year, helping you build wealth faster as your income grows. Use the Step-Up SIP Calculator to compare both scenarios side by side.

Can I save my results?

The calculators themselves don't store anything - your inputs stay in your browser. If you want to go further, create a free EnrichMe account to track your plan against your real numbers.
